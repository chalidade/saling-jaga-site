How it works

Your phone already has the radio. We just connect it.

Every phone has Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth that can talk directly to another phone, with no cell tower or internet in between. Saling Jaga uses both at once.

Three steps

From install to connected in under a minute.

Install while you have signal

Download the app now. Internet is only needed to install it and, if you like, to download the map around you.

Invite the phones around you

Open the app and other phones running Saling Jaga appear in a list. Tap “Invite” or scan a QR code.

Stay connected

Chat, talk, share location and send SOS. If someone goes out of range, their phone reconnects by itself.

The network

Five ways a message gets through.

The app picks the path itself. You just send a message as usual.

Wi‑Fi Direct

The fast path for voice, photos, video and chat within a group.

50–100 m

Bluetooth

Works with no Wi‑Fi. SOS and short messages are passed from phone to phone.

Phone to phone

Bridge phone

A phone in the middle links two groups that cannot reach each other.

+1 phone

Post Wi‑Fi

A building's Wi‑Fi or a long-range antenna unites a whole shelter area.

1–3 km

LoRa

Low-power radio devices carry SOS and short messages between groups.

km+

Walking courier

Messages travel with the people passing by.

When there is no direct path, a message is kept on the phone that received it and handed to other phones it meets along the way. Like giving a letter to someone heading in the right direction.

  • Messages are kept for up to 6 hours
  • SOS is passed on for up to 7 hops
  • Handed automatically to every Saling Jaga phone it meets

Range

From one alley to a whole valley.

Start with just two phones. When the group spreads out, the range can be extended step by step.

50–100 m

Phone to phone

Directly over Wi‑Fi Direct in the open. Shorter indoors.

+1 phone

Bridge phone

A phone in the middle taps “Become a bridge” and passes messages to those out of range.

1–3 km

Post Wi‑Fi

A building's Wi‑Fi, or a long-range Wi‑Fi antenna at a command post.

km+

LoRa gateway

Meshtastic LoRa devices carry short messages and SOS between groups.

These figures are estimates in open space. Real results depend on the phones, buildings, trees and weather.

Security

No server. The data stays in the group.

There is no account to break into and no server to go down.

Encrypted between phones

Messages are sealed with AES‑256‑GCM. There is no server and no account.

No impersonation

A message that claims to come from another member is rejected.

Passes Play Protect

Scanned by Google at install. The app has no ads and no trackers.

Verifiable

Every APK ships with a SHA‑256 to check against the downloaded file.

Honest about limits

What to know before you rely on it.

Saling Jaga complements official emergency services. It does not replace them.

It needs other phones with the app

With no other Saling Jaga phone in range, a message has nowhere to go. That is why you should bring in the people around you now.

Range depends on the place

Walls, trees and weather shorten the distance. The figures on this page are estimates in open space.

Not a replacement for emergency numbers

Saling Jaga does not call an ambulance or the fire service. It connects the people who are around you.

The next earthquake will not wait until you are ready.

Install it now while you still have signal, then bring in your family and neighbours. Saling Jaga only helps if the phones around you have it too.
