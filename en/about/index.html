About

When the signal is gone, the nearest person is the one who can help.

Saling Jaga is built in Indonesia, a country that knows earthquakes, floods and volcanoes well. The name means “looking after each other”. Our slogan: Connecting in Crisis.

Every time a major disaster strikes, the same thing happens: the power goes out, the cell towers go down, and millions of phones that are still switched on cannot be used to send news.

Yet every phone has a radio that can talk directly to the phone next to it. What was missing was an app that makes this as easy as a regular group chat, in our own language, and ready for grandparents and children alike.

That is Saling Jaga. The name is how it works: neighbours look after neighbours, a group looks after its members, and every phone that installs it extends the reach for everyone else.

We are starting with an app that is free for the public. Next come small devices for those who are not holding a phone, and partnerships with the organisations working in the field.

Principles

Three things we will not trade away.

Free for the public

Safety should not depend on the ability to pay. The app for the public is free and has no ads.

No server, no account

We do not store messages, locations or member lists. The data lives on your own phones.

Honest about limits

We write plainly about range and about what is not finished, because people rely on this in a crisis.
