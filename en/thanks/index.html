Special thanks

Covatom was not built alone.

To everyone who tried it, reported, shared and chipped in. This page is for you.

01

The early testers

Those who installed a version still full of rough edges, tried it with family and friends, and told us what did not work. Every report became a fix.

  • The Google Tester Indonesia community
  • Testers from TesBareng
02

Supporters on Trakteer

Those who chipped in so that costs like the developer account and test devices could be covered. Thank you for believing early.

03

Open-source projects

Covatom stands on the work of many people who share what they make.

  • OpenStreetMap and its contributors, for the offline maps
  • Meshtastic, for an open LoRa network
  • ZXing, for QR codes
  • Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono, the typefaces on this site
  • three.js, for the 3D device models
04

Family and friends

Those who lent their phones for range tests, walked away holding the talk button, and listened patiently to stories about signal.

Should your name be here?

If you helped test or support Covatom and would like to be named, let us know. We will gladly add you.
