Who it's for

For anyone who cannot afford to lose touch.

Free for the public. For organisations we provide training, customisation and radio devices.

Families and neighbourhoodsHajj and umrahVolunteers and postsHikers and schoolsOrganisations

Families and neighbourhoods

Know everyone is safe, within minutes.

In an earthquake, a flood or a blackout, the first thing people look for is news of family and neighbours. Saling Jaga answers without waiting for the signal to return.

  • One-tap roll call for the whole neighbourhood
  • A meeting point with direction and distance
  • Senior mode with large buttons
  • Earthquake, flood and first-aid guides with no internet

Hajj and umrah

A separated group can still find each other.

In a crowd, mobile networks are often congested and SIM cards are not always active. Pilgrims' phones can still reach each other directly.

  • Compass to the group leader and the meeting point
  • Find a separated member through nearby phones
  • Keep children and the elderly in one list
  • The app in Bahasa Indonesia and English

In preparation: a group package for travel operators and the sBand wristband for pilgrims.

Volunteers and posts

Field coordination that does not depend on cell towers.

For the teams that go in first. Assign tasks, gather reports and find the people calling for help.

  • Search for victims with a warmer-or-colder indicator
  • A LoRa gateway to link teams and the post
  • The post's laptop joins through a browser
  • Send files and documents up to 8 MB

Hikers and schools

On the mountain, in the forest, at camp.

Places that never had signal to begin with. The group still knows where everyone is and can call for help.

  • Offline maps downloaded before you leave
  • Compass to each member of the group
  • Walkie-talkie without carrying a radio
  • Battery saver mode for long trips

Organisations

For disaster agencies, schools, travel operators and companies.

The app stays free for the public. Organisations that need more can work with us.

Training and drills

We support exercises with your team or community, from installation to an evacuation drill.

Customisation

Team Edition with command-post features. Your organisation's specific needs can be discussed.

Radio devices

LoRa gateways for a range of several kilometres, and the Saling Jaga devices we are designing.
